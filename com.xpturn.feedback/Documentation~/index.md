# xpTURN.Feedback documentation

Start with Getting Started to make a first feedback, then read Concepts once: it explains what a player does with its
items and why a value looks the way it does during an effect. Keep the two references open while you work.

- [Getting Started](GettingStarted.md) — your first feedback step by step, then your own scene, the editor's tools
  and the samples
- [Concepts](Concepts.md) — targets and motions, how values are written, time and playback, Unity Timeline
- [Scripting API](ScriptingAPI.md) — the types you call from code, and the bases for feedbacks of your own
- [Feedback reference](Feedbacks.md) — every feedback, the motions it takes and what it needs
- [Performance](Performance.md) — what no allocation covers, against a coroutine per play, the cost by target

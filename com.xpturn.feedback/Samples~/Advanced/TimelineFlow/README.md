# Timeline & Flow

## Try

Play the launch: the countdown, the charge, then press Launch inside the 2 s wait (or let it launch itself). Pause, drag the slider, land it back mid-flight or from the top.

## See

Action1 flies: from the pad it plays, in flight it launches (outside the wait the signal is dropped); the mouse uses the buttons. One list (the Flight) shows the start rules — with previous, after previous, after all — a stage loop ×3 and a signal wait; its Player Control items start the endless Flame forward and stop it on the way back. Backward is a time mirror. Seek works on a nominal play only: an early Launch cuts the wait short and the play leaves its axis, so the slider stops. Loop count is the total passes; an item's repeat is extra runs. The bottom timeline is the sample's table of the list and its own clock — the play's position has no public read. Before leaves out the motion and the camera; the countdown, the pad light, the flame's show and the flow still run (out of every category).

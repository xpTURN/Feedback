# Accessibility

## Try

Hit the dummy light, heavy and critical; turn screen shake and motion down, reduce or turn off flashes, turn hit stop and rumble off — and hit again.

## See

Each setting is a category of the package: screen shake the Camera category's scale, motion Motion's, flashes the per-second limit or the Flash category off, hit stop Time, rumble Haptics. A scale reaches a running play at once (watch the banner); a category turned off leaves the item out of new plays, and a stop already begun stays. The kit writes them, composed with the HUD's Before and intensity (the panel shows what is in effect), and puts them back when the sample closes — settings last the session only.

## Controls

Space · 1 · 2 · 3 · Gamepad South · West · North — light · heavy · critical · V · C · D-pad up · down — row · Z · X · D-pad left · right — value · Mouse — menu

## HUD

Tab · R3 — feedback on/off (Before/After)
[ ] · LB RB — intensity
, . · LT RT — time scale
Esc · Start · Select — back to the hub
H · L3 — fold the HUD

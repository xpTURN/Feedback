# PostFX Lab

## Try

Pick an effect and play it, then the hit, the slow-mo and the low-health pulse — and stack them.

## See

Each item weighs its own overlay volume above the scene's volume; the later prepared sits on top (the hit over the pulse). The slow-mo's desaturation runs on the unscaled clock. Leaving the scene reverts every effect. Tab turns every category off — the slow-mo too.

## Controls

1 2 3 · Gamepad South West North — hit · slow-mo · low health · Z X C · D-pad — pick · play · V — stop all

## HUD

Tab · R3 — feedback on/off (Before/After)
[ ] · LB RB — intensity
, . · LT RT — time scale
Esc · Start · Select — back to the hub
H · L3 — fold the HUD

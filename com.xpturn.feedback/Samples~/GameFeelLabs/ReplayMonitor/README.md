# Replay Monitor

## Try

Press 1 to freeze the picture on a hit, 2 to jump between chapters and 3 for an instant replay. Z turns the monitor off and on.

## See

One Video Player driven by Play, Pause and Seek items beside flashes and shakes. X holds a frame at 4 s; C stops with Revert and the picture plays on from there, because a Seek is not undone. V restores the first state. Video clips do not play on Web.

## Controls

1 — hit freeze · 2 — chapter · 3 — instant replay · Z — power · X — hold · C — stop with Revert · V — restore

## HUD

Tab · R3 — feedback on/off (Before/After)
[ ] · LB RB — intensity
, . · LT RT — time scale
Esc · Start · Select — back to the hub
H · L3 — fold the HUD

## Requires

Video module
